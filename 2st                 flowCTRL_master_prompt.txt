MASTER PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT PROMPT

Project Name:
flowCTRL (fC)

Tagline:
"Control over your career."

Product Category:
Premium Career Intelligence + Opportunity Management SaaS Platform

Vision:
Build flowCTRL (fC) as a production-grade, premium SaaS ecosystem that helps students, fresh graduates, working professionals, career switchers, and self-taught developers take complete control over their career journey.

The platform should act as a centralized career operating system where users can:

- Discover suitable career paths
- Understand required skills
- Analyze their current skill gaps
- Generate personalized learning roadmaps
- Build professional portfolios
- Improve resumes
- Prepare for interviews
- Find jobs and internships
- Connect with mentors
- Join communities
- Track growth and achievements

The core mission is:

"Bridge the gap between skills and opportunities while helping people discover, build, and accelerate their careers."
